Patient support programs

Who, why & how.

At Jusoor every PSP is unique and is customised to the nature of the disease, the patient segment, the treatment journey, how patients are distributed and the approved content. These are the forms it takes.

01.

Patient Educational Programs

A face-to-face team alongside tailored call-centre services, matched to each patient category and each disease area, giving patients the support they need throughout their treatment journey.

02.

Care Lines

Contact-centre services that give patients and consumers help and support on any aspect of the disease, the brand or the product.

03.

Patient Adherence Programs

Educational, encouraging programs delivered by contact-centre agents or face-to-face team members — explaining the treatment and how to use it, with periodic dosage reminders.

04.

Patient Access Programs

For patients who cannot reach the treatment they need because of financial, insurance or logistical barriers.

05.

Home Nursing

For patients who struggle to take their medication because of distance or the state of their illness — extending to home blood draws and lab sampling, screening tests and vaccination.

06.

After-Sales Programs

Continuing service for products that need extended or ongoing training and support after they reach the patient.

Programs

Programs we design, deliver and operate.

Specialized chronic-disease care

Risk stratification, guideline-cited AI care plans, coordinator workflows and outcome reporting. Live, demonstrable diabetes and kidney (CKD) platforms — with eGFR-slope risk prediction, medication-gap engines, Arabic after-visit summaries and a virtual clinic. The same engine extends to hypertension, cardiovascular disease and obesity.

Patient support & adherence (PSP)

Clinically governed, physician-led adherence programs: detecting treatment discontinuation, engaging patients through Arabic AI-dialect outreach, escalating clinically when needed, and reporting refill persistence, re-initiation rates and real-world evidence back to the sponsor.

Registries-as-a-Service

Turnkey national disease and product registries: protocol, governance framework, data dictionary, data-quality management, clinical adjudication, analytics and publication support — run as a managed service.

Vaccination coverage & uptake

An AI clinical engine reads patient cohorts, identifies every missing vaccination and converts it into a care-team action — then analyses uptake data and the barriers behind low coverage, with reporting to the sponsor and health authorities.

Market access, RWE & post-market surveillance

Clinical epidemiology, biostatistics and regulatory writing: market-access dossiers covering disease burden, treatment patterns and economic value; real-world-evidence study design and analysis; and SFDA-compliant post-market surveillance and pharmacovigilance reporting.

Clinical AI for hospital information systems

Guideline-cited decision support embeddable in any HIS or EMR, running fully locally and offline. Proven predictive models — safe-discharge, readmission risk, AI triage, remote monitoring — plus hospital efficiency intelligence: length-of-stay reduction, emergency-flow optimisation, virtual-first ED pathways, and supply-chain optimization.
